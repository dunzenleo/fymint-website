1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They can help websites remember preferences, provide functionality, understand usage, and support advertising.

Preference cookies: remember settings and preferences.

Analytics cookies: help us understand visitors, pages viewed, traffic sources, and performance.

Advertising cookies: may support advertisements, measurement, frequency controls, and relevant advertising.
